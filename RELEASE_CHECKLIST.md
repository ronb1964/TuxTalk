# TuxTalk Release Checklist

Ron's checks at Step 8 of the release process in CLAUDE.md. Show him only what the
VM run (Step 4b) can't check, as a checklist panel built from these items.

First run (since 0.14.2 it recommends one setup; CUDA is not offered here):
- [ ] Welcome screen appears, and its card names the graphics card and language ("Parakeet on your NVIDIA ..., For English")
- [ ] Other options: changing the language, model or graphics-card tickbox updates the card and its download size. The language list reads "Spanish (Español)", "Japanese (日本語)" and so on, with no flags; Preferences → General → Change model, device or language → Language shows the same list
- [ ] Welcome screen's "How It Works" reads 1. Hold F8 (or a key you pick next), 2. Talk, 3. Let go. Hotkey test, then straight to the downloads: no "You're All Set!" screen before them. The hotkey test works without a mouse: Tab to Change Key, Space, press the new key, Tab on to Continue
- [ ] Downloads show progress bars: the Vulkan engine and Parakeet for the graphics card. The log must show a real download, not "cached": "Parakeet Ultra downloaded for vulkan"
- [ ] The speed check runs, and the log shows its result (`Speed check (parakeet-v3): graphics …s, processor …s`)
- [ ] Default settings are correct (model=parakeet-v3, device=vulkan when the card won, auto_period=True, auto_timeout 5 min, language_mode=auto)
- [ ] Dictation works on the graphics card (log: `Parakeet on GPU`, and "Parakeet on the graphics card from ggml-parakeet-ultra-q8_0.bin")
- [ ] Parakeet Ultra offer (a VM with the original Parakeet from 0.16, updated to this version without fresh-start): about 20 s after start, "Parakeet got more accurate" with Not Now and Download (669 MB, or 668 MB on the processor). Download shows a progress bar, dictation restarts by itself (log: "Parakeet Ultra downloaded", "Parakeet on the graphics card from ggml-parakeet-ultra-q8_0.bin") and dictates. Restart TuxTalk: no second offer. On another VM, Not Now: dictation keeps working on the original Parakeet and the offer never comes back, but Preferences → General → Speech Recognition shows "A more accurate Parakeet is available." with Update Parakeet (669 MB, or 668 MB on the processor); it downloads, dictation restarts onto Ultra, and the row is gone next time Preferences opens. About TuxTalk credits Moondream, NVIDIA and Olicorne
- [ ] Hotkey helper (default since 0.16): first-run's Fix Typing asks for the password once, says no restart is needed, and F8 works straight away without the user joining the input group (`systemctl is-active tuxtalk-hotkeys`, `getent group input`; log: "Hotkeys come from the TuxTalk hotkey helper"). After a reboot it starts by itself. In a VM, also once on an immutable distro (Bazzite or Silverblue) and once without systemd, where it falls back to the input group (log: "Hotkey helper not used") and the setup screen and Preferences say why, with the 🔒 line about programs reading the keyboard
- [ ] Stopped hotkey helper (a VM with the helper, out of the input group): `sudo systemctl stop tuxtalk-hotkeys`, then tray → Restart Service: F8 does nothing, and Preferences → Advanced → Show system check says the helper "is installed but isn't answering". Update asks for the password once; F8 then dictates without restarting TuxTalk (log: "Hotkey helper connected again")
- [ ] Keyboard plugged in during the hotkey test (a VM without the helper, in the input group): start first-run with no USB keyboard passed through, open the hotkey test ("TuxTalk can't read the keyboard yet" shows), then pass one through: within a few seconds the notice goes and F8 on it shows "✓ Working!" (log: "Hotkey test: reading keyboard …, plugged in meanwhile"). Take it out again: the notice comes back and a key reads "✓ Seen, not active yet"

- [ ] No system tray (Silverblue VM, plain GNOME): the welcome screen's "Install GNOME Extension" is ticked, with the amber "this desktop has no system tray" line. Untick it and finish setup: TuxTalk's menu window appears (log: "No system tray and no extension running"). Opening TuxTalk from the apps again brings it back (log: "Opened the menu window (no system tray here)"); its buttons work, and the switch turns dictation off and on. Right-click TuxTalk in the dash: Preferences, Transcribe a File, Help, Quit TuxTalk. On KDE, opening TuxTalk again shows the same window without the no-tray note
- [ ] Press enter: in a chat box (or KWrite) dictate "see you at noon, press enter": types "See you at noon." then a real Enter (log: 'Pressed Enter (said "press enter")'). Saying only "press enter" just presses Enter. "press enter to continue" mid-sentence is typed as words; "new line" still makes a line without sending. Preferences → Advanced → Text → "Send each dictation automatically (press Enter)" (off, "Send: After 2 seconds" greyed until ticked; choices Right away to After 4 seconds). Ticked, in a chat box: "Sending in 2 s. Tap F8 to hold it." floats over the lower part of the window, just above the message box, counts down 2, 1 (with 4 seconds: 4, 3, 2, 1), then Enter (log: "Sending in 2s", "Pressed Enter"). Tap F8 during the wait: "Not sent. Press Enter when you're ready." and nothing is sent (log: "Held the send"); tap F9 the same, with no recording started. Hold F8 during the wait and keep talking: that dictation is added after a space ("delay. All right", not "delay.All") and then sent. Switch windows during the wait: the countdown stops at once with "Not sent: you switched windows." and nothing is sent (log: "Not sent: another window has the focus now"); on KDE and GNOME also between two KWrite windows maximized on one screen, while moving the window during the wait still sends. Click into another chat window while a long dictation is still going in: the text stays and nothing is sent, with the same notice (log: "Not sent: the focus moved while the words were delivered"). "Right away" sends at once; with Right Ctrl as the toggle key, a tap while the Enter waits for the key holds it there too. Open Preferences during the wait: nothing is sent ("Not sent: a TuxTalk window has the focus."). On GNOME updated from 0.18, before logging out and in, nothing is sent automatically ("Not sent: this desktop doesn't say which window has the focus. Press Enter when you're ready."), as on COSMIC, while "..., press enter" still presses it. In KWrite (or LibreOffice Writer) nothing is sent and no new line appears (log: "Not sending: Enter only starts a new line in this app"); saying "press enter" there still presses it. Tap the hotkey sooner" floats over the window (log: "Voice edit not done: already_sent_auto"). "meet me at noon and" (let go after "and") is typed with no period and not sent, and "Not sent: it ended on “and”. Press Enter when you're ready." floats over the window (log: 'Held back the Enter: the dictation ended on "and"'); the next dictation carries on in lowercase. "I want to play around with" is sent
- [ ] Press tab: in KWrite dictate "name press tab address": types "Name", a real tab, then "address."
